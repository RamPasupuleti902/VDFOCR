How it works:

1. Upload the eSIM card photo.
2. The QR code is scanned to get the LPA / activation data.
3. The barcode is scanned to get the ICCID.
4. OCR reads the printed PIN and PUK.
5. Results are displayed in the table.

Processing happens in your browser. The image is not uploaded to a server by this application.
